Good to know

  • Family plan: the whole family deductible and family out-of-pocket maximum apply.
  • Inpatient stays: HMS must be contacted before a planned admission, or within 48 hours of an emergency or maternity admission. Check that your provider did it.
  • At the pharmacy before your deductible is met, you pay Highmark’s discounted price, and it counts toward the deductible.
  • Out-of-network providers can balance bill you. That is not capped by the plan.
  • Hospital-based offices can charge a facility fee on top of the professional fee.
